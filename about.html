ABOUT MODERN SAVES

Your internet, remembered.

Modern Saves is a modern bookmark manager built to make saving and finding things from the internet simple.

THE IDEA

The internet is full of useful things. Remembering where they are shouldn't be difficult.

Every day we discover articles, videos, products, tutorials, websites and ideas that we want to come back to later. Traditional bookmarks can quickly turn into long lists that become difficult to search.

Modern Saves was created around a simple idea: save now, find later.

WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

A personal memory for the internet.

Modern Saves helps turn the things you discover online into a personal, searchable library.

Save anything

Save websites, articles, videos, products, ideas and anything else you want to remember.

Organize simply

Keep your saves organized without making the saving process complicated.

Find it later

Search through your saved content instead of trying to remember where you found something.

Search with AI

AI Search helps you find saved content based on what you mean, not only the exact words you remember.

OUR VISION

Make the internet easier to remember.

We want Modern Saves to become the place where useful things from the internet are saved, remembered and found again.

Instead of opening dozens of tabs, forgetting useful websites, or searching through endless bookmark folders, your internet memory should be easy to access.
