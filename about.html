THE PROBLEM

The internet is full of useful things. Finding them later shouldn't be difficult.

Every day we come across articles, videos, products, ideas, tutorials and websites that we want to come back to. But bookmarks can quickly become long lists that are difficult to organize and search.

OUR IDEA

Save first. Organize and find later.

Later, you can search through your collection and use AI Search to discover saved content based on what you remember about it.
