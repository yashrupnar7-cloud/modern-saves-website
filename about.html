ABOUT MODERN SAVES

Your internet, remembered.

Modern Saves is a Chrome bookmark manager built for the things you discover online, want to remember, and don't want to lose.

THE PROBLEM

The internet is full of useful things. Finding them later shouldn't be difficult.

Every day we come across articles, videos, products, tutorials, ideas and websites that we want to come back to. But traditional bookmarks can quickly become long lists that are difficult to organize and search.

WHAT IS MODERN SAVES?

A better way to manage your bookmarks in Chrome.

Modern Saves is a bookmark manager and Chrome extension that gives you one place to save, organize and search the useful things you find on the web.

Instead of letting saved links become a forgotten list, Modern Saves turns them into a personal library you can return to whenever you need something.

OUR IDEA

Save first. Organize and find later.

Modern Saves makes saving something from the web simple. Instead of stopping to build complicated folders every time you find something useful, just save it.

Later, you can organize your collection, search through your saved content, or use AI Search to find something based on what you remember about it.

WHY MODERN SAVES?

More than a traditional bookmark list.

Browser bookmarks are useful for keeping links. Modern Saves is designed around a different idea: your saved internet content should be easy to organize, search and rediscover.

Whether you're saving something to learn later, a product you want to buy, a video you want to watch, or an article you want to revisit, Modern Saves gives those discoveries a place in your personal internet library.

AI BOOKMARK MANAGER

Find what you saved by remembering the idea.

Sometimes you remember what a page was about but not its exact title. AI Search helps you search your saved content using natural language and meaning.

For example, you might search for "that website about learning Python" instead of trying to remember the original page title.
